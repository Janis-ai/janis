# Investor pitch — VO script (timed to "Michael's video (1)" / janis-pitch-cropped.mp4)

Read straight through — each block starts where its beat lands on screen.

**0:00 — Inbox + copilot**
"This is Janis. Every customer conversation a business has — web chat, Messenger, email, SMS, voice — landing in one queue. And it's a copilot for the operator too: ask it anything about your setup, it answers from your real config."

**0:20 — Storefront, greeting + teaser**
"This is Janis inside a real Shopify storefront — live catalog, live pricing, inside the chat bubble. It greets the shopper before they even ask."

**0:35 — Browse ask → collections picker**
"'Show me the snowboards you have.' Not a canned answer — that's a live query against the store. It offers collections first — six products, counted the same way the cards filter them."

**0:50 — Cards + sale ask**
"Real products, real prices, as tappable cards. 'Are any on sale?' — it finds the markdown, strikes through the old price, shows the sale badge."

**1:05 — Options → Add to cart → checkout**
"See options, pick a color — add to cart, right in the thread. And a checkout card closes the loop in-chat — no redirect maze, no lost session."

**1:15 — Return ask begins**
"Now the ask AI shouldn't guess on — a cracked board, a return, 'can I talk to a person?'"

**1:25 — Messenger, same agent**
"And here's the same agent on Facebook Messenger — same brain, same catalog, rendered native. 'Browse snowboards' — cards, prices, View product. Every channel a business already lives on."

**1:45 — The alert in the inbox**
"Inside Janis, that return request is an open alert — flagged, summarized, routed. The operator sees the whole thread: cards sent, cart contents, every turn."

**2:00 — Takeover**
"One click and a human steps in — same thread, full context. Nobody repeats themselves. Watch the reply get typed…"

**2:25 — Back on the store**
"—and back on the store, the human's answer lands in the same bubble. 'Bob here — a real person.' AI when it's best. Humans when they're needed. One seamless conversation."

**2:40 — Rescue → regression test**
"And every rescue lands here. Flagged, fix drafted, one click makes it a regression test — replayed against every future change. The product gets better because it was used. That's the flywheel."

**2:55 — Approvals**
"And actions that touch money wait for a human — request a return, create a discount code, approval-gated by policy. The AI proposes; the business approves."

**3:10 — Channels + close**
"Web bubble, Messenger, WhatsApp, email, SMS — connected in minutes, one agent, one brain. And here's the part you can't buy: seventeen thousand businesses signed up for the classic Janis inside Slack. Migrating is one click — agents, channels, credentials come with them. Distribution is a rail, not an ad spend."
