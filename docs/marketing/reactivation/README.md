# Reactivation sequence — legacy Janis customers

Audience: ~17k legacy customers exported from the old platform (see RUNBOOK
for the export path). Goal: re-introduce Janis as the approvals-first AI
customer-service layer — "AI proposes, a human approves, the customer never
knows the difference."

Three emails, one week apart. Subject lines are candidates — test in the send
tool. All CTAs land on https://app.janis.ai?utm_source=reactivation&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=winback-N.

## Email 1 — "we're back, differently"

**Subject:** Janis is back — AI that asks before it acts
**Preview:** Your chatbot now proposes refunds, a human approves, the customer never knows.

The pitch: the AI does the work — drafts replies, looks up orders, proposes
refunds — but anything that touches money or policy goes to a human for
one-tap approval inside the same conversation. The customer sees one seamless
thread. No bot roulette, no rogue refunds.

One paragraph, one screenshot/GIF of the approval card, one CTA: "See it run
on your own site" → app.janis.ai. Sign-off honest about the gap: Janis went
quiet for a while; this is what it became.

## Email 2 — "the rescue loop"

**Subject:** Your AI learns every time a human saves it
**Preview:** Every takeover becomes a regression test. Permanently.

The differentiator #2: when a human takes over a conversation, Janis turns
that rescue into a regression test automatically — the fix can't silently
revert, and knowledge gaps get surfaced as articles to write. Nobody else
closes that loop.

CTA: "Watch a rescue become a test" → app.janis.ai/docs?guide=automation (or
the demo). Short. This email is for the burned-by-dumb-bots segment.

## Email 3 — "everything in one inbox" + deadline

**Subject:** Last note — Messenger, WhatsApp, email, Slack, one inbox
**Preview:** And your existing agents can plug in — BYOK welcome.

The unification pitch: web chat, Messenger, Instagram, WhatsApp, email, SMS,
voice, Slack — one inbox, humans take over anywhere, hosted or bring-your-own
agent via API/Zapier. Close with a soft deadline ("founding-rate pricing ends
<date>") only if pricing actually gates — otherwise end with a plain
"reply and I'll set you up personally" CTA; replies are the metric that matters.

## Rules

- Suppress anyone who opts out (STOP/unsubscribe → suppression table —
  enforcement is in the campaign sender already).
- One send per contact — dedupe on email before enqueue.
- Replies route to the founder inbox — that's the channel; answer personally.
